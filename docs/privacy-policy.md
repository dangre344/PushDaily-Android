# Privacy Policy for Push Daily Home Workout

**Last updated: October 09, 2026**

## Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use the Service and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

We use Your Personal Data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

**In short:**

- Your **period tracking data, workout history and water intake are stored only on your phone**. They are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone.
- Some **profile details** (such as height, weight, age and gender) are shared with our analytics provider, and with our AI service when you use the AI trainer. Section *"Health and Fitness Information"* below explains exactly when and why.
- **Camera images used for push-up counting are processed on your device** and are never uploaded or stored.
- **We do not sell your personal data.**

## Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

### Types of Data Collected

#### Personal Data

When You set up Your profile, We collect:

- Name
- Gender
- Age
- Height and weight
- Fitness level and fitness goal
- Preferred workout days and reminder time

The Service does not ask for Your email address, phone number or password.

#### Health and Fitness Information

| Data | Where it is stored | Leaves your device? |
|---|---|---|
| **Period tracking** (period start dates, cycle length, period length, delay history) | On Your Device only | **No.** Never uploaded to our servers, never sent to analytics, never shared with third parties. |
| **Workout history** (exercises done, dates, calories) | On Your Device only | **No**, except a summary of it if You ask the AI trainer to review Your progress (see "AI Trainer"). |
| **Water intake** (glasses logged, daily goal) | On Your Device only | **No.** |
| **Profile body details** (height, weight, age, gender, goal, fitness level) | On Your Device | **Yes, in limited cases:** shared with Our analytics provider (Mixpanel) to understand how the app is used, and sent to Our AI service when You use the AI trainer, so its advice fits You. |
| **Push-up score** (count, display name, country) | Our leaderboard server | **Yes**, if You complete a push-up session. It is used to show the public daily leaderboard (see "Push-Up Challenge"). |

Period tracking is optional. You can use the app without it, and You can delete any entry at any time from the Period Tracking screen.

#### Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically and may include:

- Device type, model and a device or advertising identifier
- Operating system and app version
- App usage behavior (screens visited, features used, session duration)
- Crash and error reports
- How You found the app (for example, an Instagram or advertising link), via the Google Play Install Referrer

### Camera

The app asks for camera access only for these features, and only when You use them:

- **Push-up counter:** the camera is used to count Your push-ups. Images are processed **on Your Device in real time** and are **never recorded, stored or uploaded**. Only the final push-up count is saved.
- **Food label scanner:** if You scan a barcode, the barcode number is looked up in the public Open Food Facts database. If You take a photo of a nutrition label, that photo is sent to Our AI service to read the label. The photo is used only to produce Your result and is not kept by Us afterwards.

We do NOT collect:

- Contacts
- Precise location (GPS)
- Your photo gallery
- Microphone audio

### AI Trainer ("Jack")

When You chat with the AI trainer, or ask it to review Your progress, We send to Our AI service:

- Your message
- Your profile details (gender, age, height, weight, goal, fitness level), so the advice is relevant to You
- For a progress review: a summary of Your workout history

Our AI service forwards this to an AI model provider (currently Google Gemini, with Groq as a backup) to generate a reply. **Period tracking data is never included.** AI replies are general fitness guidance, not medical advice.

### Push-Up Challenge (Public Leaderboard)

If You complete a push-up session, We store on Our server Your push-up count, Your display name (the name on Your profile), Your country (taken from Your device's region setting, not GPS) and a random app ID. The daily top results, including display names, are **visible to other users** of the app. If You prefer, change Your profile name before taking part.

### Push Notifications and Reminders

With Your permission, We may send You:

- Workout reminders
- Daily fitness notifications
- Water reminders
- **Period reminders**, which remind You to log a period date around the expected date or every 15 days. They are scheduled on Your Device and never contain dates or cycle details, so nothing sensitive shows on Your lock screen.

To deliver push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns Your Device a notification token. You can turn reminders off inside the app or disable notifications anytime from Your device settings.

### Home-Screen Widget

If You add the Push Daily widget, it shows Your first name and current workout streak on Your home screen. This information stays on Your Device.

### Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use Your device's advertising identifier to show personalized ads and to measure ad performance. You can reset Your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Health and Fitness Information, including period data, is never shared with advertisers.

### Payments and Support

"Buy me a coffee" contributions are completely optional and are processed by Your UPI app or by Buy Me a Coffee on their own secure pages. **We never see or store Your card details, UPI PIN or bank details.** Those services have their own privacy policies.

## Use of Your Personal Data

We use Your data to:

- Provide and maintain the app and personalize Your workouts
- Send the reminders You have turned on
- Run the AI trainer and the push-up leaderboard
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Analyze usage trends and understand which features help users
- Measure which marketing channels bring new users
- Fix bugs and crashes, and optimize features
- Show ads that keep the app free

## Sharing Your Personal Data

We share data only with these Service Providers, and only for the purposes described above:

| Service Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Mixpanel | Product analytics | App usage, app ID, profile details (name, gender, age, height, weight, goal, fitness level), install source |
| Google Firebase (Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config) | Crash reports, push notifications, app configuration | Crash logs, device info, app ID, notification token |
| Google AdMob | Advertising | Advertising ID, device info, ad interactions |
| Google Gemini / Groq (through our Cloudflare server) | AI trainer replies, food label reading | Chat messages, profile details, workout summary, label photos |
| Supabase (through our Cloudflare server) | Push-up leaderboard | Display name, push-up count, country, app ID |
| Open Food Facts | Barcode lookup | The barcode number only |
| Google Play | App updates, reviews, install source | As described in Google's policies |

We may also disclose data:

- For legal compliance
- During business transfers (if applicable)

**We do NOT sell Your personal data.** We never share period tracking data with anyone.

## Retention of Your Personal Data

- **Data stored on Your Device** (period tracking, workout history, water intake, profile) stays until You delete it in the app, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- **Leaderboard scores** are kept to show daily and all-time results, until You ask Us to delete them.
- **Analytics and crash data** are kept by Our providers for as long as needed to analyze trends and fix problems, and in line with their retention settings.
- **AI requests** are used to produce Your reply and are not stored by Us afterwards.

## Transfer of Your Personal Data

Our Service Providers may process data on servers outside India. We use providers that apply appropriate security measures to protect it.

## Delete Your Personal Data

You can:

- Delete individual period entries from the Period Tracking screen
- Clear all on-device data from Your device settings (Apps → Push Daily → Storage → Clear data)
- Uninstall the app, which removes all data stored on Your Device
- Email Us to delete Your leaderboard scores and analytics data. Include Your profile name and We will respond within 30 days.
